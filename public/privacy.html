Privacy Policy
How we collect, use, and protect your information
Last updated: May 29, 2026  ·  Effective immediately
Overview

Involux ("we", "us", or "our") is an invoice management platform that helps businesses automatically collect, organize, and track their invoices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our services at involux.ca.

By using Involux, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We use this to create and identify your account.

Business information: The business name(s) you create within Involux to organize your invoices.

Invoice data: Files you upload (PDFs, images) and the data extracted from them — including supplier names, amounts, invoice numbers, and dates.

Gmail data (if you connect Gmail): If you choose to connect your Gmail account, we access:

  • Email metadata (sender, date, subject) of emails that contain invoice attachments
  • Email attachments (PDF and image files) that appear to be invoices
  • We do not read the body text of your emails
  • We do not access emails that do not contain invoice attachments

Gmail Data Use: Involux's use of data obtained through Google APIs, including Gmail data, is limited to providing and improving the invoice import feature. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with any third parties except as necessary to operate the service (e.g., using OpenAI to extract invoice data from attachments). Our use of Gmail data complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How We Use Your Information
  • To provide the Involux service — organizing, storing, and displaying your invoices
  • To automatically scan your Gmail inbox for invoice attachments (only if you connect Gmail)
  • To extract invoice data (supplier, amount, date, invoice number) using AI (OpenAI GPT-4o)
  • To allow you to share invoice access with your accountant
  • To maintain and improve the platform
  • To communicate with you about your account if necessary
How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only in the following limited cases:

  • Supabase: Our database and file storage provider. Invoice files and data are stored securely in Supabase.
  • OpenAI: Invoice attachment files are sent to OpenAI's API solely to extract invoice data (supplier, amount, date). OpenAI does not use this data to train their models per their API data usage policy.
  • Google: We use Google OAuth for sign-in and the Gmail API for inbox scanning. Your tokens are stored securely and never shared.
  • Your accountant: Only if you explicitly send them an invite through the Share Access feature.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.
Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted storage and row-level security. Invoice files are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to your account.

Gmail refresh tokens are stored encrypted and are used solely to scan your inbox for invoice attachments on your behalf. We use HTTPS for all data transmission.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your business and all associated invoices at any time from within the app.

Your Rights & Choices
  • Disconnect Gmail: You can revoke Involux's Gmail access at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing Involux.
  • Delete your data: You can delete your business and all invoices from the Settings page in the app.
  • Account deletion: To fully delete your account and all data, contact us at the email below.
  • Data access: You can export your invoice data at any time using the Export CSV feature in the app.
Children's Privacy

Involux is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Involux after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
